What's Happening at HCC

Sunday Worship

Come worship with us! Service begins at 10:00 am.
About the Worship Service

Catechism Classes

A thirty minute study of doctrine with the New City Catechism. We have separate classes for adults, middle school, elementary, and nursery. Each Sunday at 9:00 am before the Worship Service.

Home Groups

Our Home Groups focus on Bible study, prayer, and fellowship. Home Groups meet every other Thursday from 7:30 - 9:30 pm in Wheaton and West Chicago.

Women's Fellowship & Prayer

The ladies of HCC meet for fellowship and intentional prayer for our church and for one another. We meet on the first and third Saturday of each month at 7:00 am in a Wheaton-area home.

Sunday Nights for Teens

Evening hangouts in homes for teens. Sunday nights from 7:00 - 9:30 pm. Locations to be announced each week.

Men's Prayer & Breakfast

The men of HCC gather monthly to pray and eat breakfast.
