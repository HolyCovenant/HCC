Gathering. Proclaiming. Rejoicing.

We are a warmly inviting Christian congregation, committed to Scripture, fellowship, the sacraments, and prayer.

Our desire is for men, women, and children to be encouraged in Christian faith through the proclamation of God's Word and full participation in Church.

Come taste and see the goodness of the Lord!

When and Where

We meet at Crossway Publishers in Wheaton. Please park behind the building and enter the back door. Sunday school is at 9:00 AM, service begins at 10:00 and ends around 11:15.
