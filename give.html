Giving

Members of HCC have the privilege and responsibility to participate in and contribute to the life of the Church by using their God-given time, talent, and treasure to build up and serve the Church body. Members commit to supporting the vision and values of the Church. One way is by giving the first-fruits of our income in tithes and offerings.

We have a collection box in the back during services, or you can set up an online giving account here.

Alternatively, you can mail your offering to:
Holy Covenant Church
223 E. Seminary Ave.
Wheaton, IL 60187

“Honor the LORD with your wealth
and with the firstfruits of all your produce.”

Proverbs 3:9 (ESV)
